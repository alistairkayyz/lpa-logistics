# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

South African and regional business decision-makers (procurement managers, supply chain leads, business owners) who need reliable road freight for domestic distribution or cross-border logistics into the SADC region. They are evaluating transport partners and need to feel confident in LPA's credibility and capability before reaching out. They are typically on desktop but may check on mobile from a warehouse or logistics environment.

## Product Purpose

LPA Logistics is a marketing and lead-generation website for a South African road freight company. Its purpose is to present LPA as a professional, credible transport partner and convert visitor interest into quote enquiries via the contact form. Success is measured by quality of enquiries submitted.

## Brand Personality

Professional, dependable, no-nonsense. Three words: **reliable, grounded, accountable**. The brand communicates confidence through precision and specificity — not hype. The tone is direct and practical, like a trusted operations manager rather than a marketer.

## Anti-references

- Overly animated startup/tech SaaS sites (too flashy for a logistics operation)
- Generic "blue corporate" sites that feel interchangeable
- Sites with aggressive pop-ups, ticker banners, or lead-capture walls
- Logistics sites that look like they haven't been updated since 2015

## Design Principles

1. **Specificity over superlatives** — real routes, real countries, real fleet details beat claims like "world-class service"
2. **Credibility through structure** — clear information hierarchy and professional layout do more than hero animations
3. **Every pixel earns trust** — visual polish signals that LPA runs a tight operation
4. **Mobile as a first-class surface** — prospects may visit from a phone in a warehouse or at a border post
5. **Content is always accessible** — animations enhance; they never gate or delay the message

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. All form fields must have properly associated labels. Interactive elements must have visible focus indicators. Text contrast must meet 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. The site must be operable by keyboard. Animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
